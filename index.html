Our Story

A home in the hills,
always waiting for you

Misthaven was born from a simple dream — to share the quiet magic of Ooty with every traveller who arrives at our wooden door. We're not a hotel. We're a home.

Our family has lived in these hills for generations. We'll wake you with chai, feed you our grandmother's recipes, and take you to corners of the Nilgiris that no guidebook has found.

Beyond the Room

Local Experiences

We'll take you deep into the Nilgiris — experiences curated by locals, for curious travellers.

🍵
Tea Estate Walk

Walk through rolling tea gardens at dawn, pluck fresh leaves, and learn the art of orthodox tea from our estate manager.

🚂
Nilgiri Mountain Railway

Board the UNESCO heritage toy train from Ooty to Coonoor — a journey through tunnels, bridges, and breathtaking scenery.

🌿
Tribal Forest Trek

A guided trek through Mudumalai reserve with a Toda tribal guide who knows every plant, bird call, and hidden waterfall.

🍲
Family Kitchen Session

Cook traditional Nilgiri dishes with our host — venison curry, wild honey cake, and cardamom rice — using garden-fresh ingredients.
